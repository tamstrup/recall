# Things that need you

No credentials, paid services, Xcode capability changes, or microphone access are required.

## Check a recording from your own device

- **What:** Verify transcription and speaker separation on a representative Sony, iPhone, or Plaud recording. The automated audio fixture cannot establish quality on your microphones, room acoustics, or voices.
- **Steps:** Open `build/Recall.app`. Drag a recording from Finder into the window. Let first-use model preparation finish, then compare a few passages with playback. Click a speaker name to rename it, and use Notes → Meeting Minutes → Generate.
- **Blocks development:** No. This is the remaining real-world acceptance check.
